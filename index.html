Swarna And The Magical Forest

Meet The Characters

Swarna and the Magical Forest is a collection of children's stories about a little girl and her adventures in a magical forest. With simple characters and delightful stories, it makes for a perfect bedtime read for children.

Come, let's get to know some of our characters!

Swarna

Also known as Tara, Swarna is a naughty six-year-old who is brave and fearless. She is a kind but lonely child who believes there is good in everyone. She sees the world with eyes full of wonder and, therefore, finds friends wherever she goes.

But does that mean she always stays out of trouble? Not at all! Wait until you hear about her notorious encounter with Kalika, the most dreaded witch in the kingdom, who is known to carry children away to her pale and mysterious den! Know More

Chiku

Chiku is one of Swarna's two constant companions. He is a lively, talking bird, though Swarna has no idea what kind of bird he really is!

Ajji found him as a tiny baby, fallen from his nest in a tall tree near the forest. She nursed him and took care of him ever since.

Since the little bird could talk, Ajji decided he must be a magical bird. And what a talkative bird he is! Chiku loves to chatter, but when it comes to courage, well, that's another story. At the slightest hint of danger, he's ready to spread his wings and fly away!

Moti

Moti is a tiny little pup and Swarna's other constant companion. He may be small, but he has the heart of a lion! Like Swarna, Moti loves exploring new places. In fact, he is often the one who leads them to a mysterious spot, a hidden corner, or an unexpected adventure. With Moti around, you never know what exciting discovery lies just around the corner!

Ajji

Ajji (which means grandma in Kannada) is a loving old lady and Swarna's only human companion. She has taken care of Swarna ever since she was a baby.

Though Ajji is poor and uneducated, she has taught Swarna some of the most valuable lessons in life.

Ajji has never told Swarna what girls should or shouldn't do. And so, Swarna grows up fearless, curious, and ready to explore even the most dreaded place in the whole kingdom — the magical forest of Swarnaarayanya!
