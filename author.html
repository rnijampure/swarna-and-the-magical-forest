This is what she says about her book-.

" This book is my humble attempt to raise the interest of our young generation, in reading Indian books. The main character is a girl, who should have been is very inquisitive! Her curiosity leads her to explore the dreaded forest, allowing her to have a new adventure everyday and learn something new. The central theme of the book is that we should not let our fears drive our actions. Once you face your fears, you realise they are not as scary as you might have thought of them to be! "
-Rohini Nijampure
