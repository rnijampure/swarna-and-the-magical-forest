About the Author - Rohini N

Rohini N is a published author, writer, and web developer with a love for stories, books, and exploring new ideas. Her writing spans children's fiction, creative storytelling, educational content, and technology-related articles.

Her debut children's book, Swarna and the Magical Forest, is a collection of stories about a curious little girl and her adventures in a mysterious forest. Through simple storytelling, memorable characters, and a touch of magic, Rohini hopes to inspire young readers to discover the joy of reading and let their imaginations wander.

A Few Words from the Author

Writing Swarna and the Magical Forest was my humble attempt to encourage children to discover the joy of reading Indian stories.

At the heart of the book is a little girl who is curious about the world around her. Her curiosity leads her into the dreaded magical forest, where every adventure brings something new to discover and a lesson to learn.

Through Swarna's adventures, I hope to remind young readers that we should never let our fears decide what we can or cannot do. Sometimes, the things we fear aren't quite as frightening as we imagine them to be. All it takes is a little courage, a curious mind, and the willingness to take that first step!
